## Why the council landed here

- Broad reach on the platform key breaks the money: free tokens cost cash before anyone pays, so the broad launch is a cost with no ask (Buffett, Bezos, Naval).
- Broad reach on bring-your-own-key collapses into the niche: ordinary users have a subscription, not an API key, so the key itself filters the audience back to professionals (Graham, Elon, Sara, Martell, Hormozi).
- The moat is thin. Poe, TypingMind, OpenRouter's chat and others already do multi-model chat and the labs can add a compare button. Only repeat use shows whether the debate roles and the agreement meter are worth more than a feature (Naval, Sara, Elon, Buffett).
- Ten hours a week and no audience cap reach, not the code (Martell, Elon). Hours go to distribution, not new features.

## Where they fought

- **Business or side income.** Reach estimates ran from 3,000 to 40,000. Elon cut his figure when Martell showed that 7 clips a week need the whole week. All nine now say side income.
- **Product or feature.** Graham and Hormozi say competitors prove demand; Naval, Sara, Elon and Buffett say that proves demand for the category, not for debates. Not settled by argument: all nine make the second-debate rate the first gate.
- **Price.** Hormozi wanted EUR 79 up front, then dropped it after Sara argued nobody has seen the product used. Graham, Sara and Bezos: EUR 29 first.
- **Unargued alternatives.** Gary V argued broad clips with a narrow offer; Naval argued open source as a companion. Both were folded into the resolution, neither beat B.

## Minority report

Hormozi: the higher price is the only way this becomes a business, and testing it late costs months. At 60 payers, EUR 29 is EUR 1,700 a month and EUR 79 is EUR 4,700. What would have to be true: strangers pay EUR 79 after a live demo at a close rate close to EUR 29's. Naval: the app is a feature the labs can copy; the real asset is the founder's name, so publish the debates before building anything. Graham and Sara add that their own lens (venture growth, retail) fits a solo software side income poorly.

## Conditions

1. EUR 150 a month ceiling on platform-key tokens and a per-user daily limit, in code, abuse-tested before any public link. When the ceiling is hit, the platform key switches off (Buffett).
2. User API keys encrypted at rest, with a passing test that another user cannot read them (Buffett).
3. Free tier: bring your own key plus one capped trial debate. One paid tier at EUR 29, no Workspace tier.
4. Debate transcripts public from day one. Decide about releasing the code at day 90.

## What to do next

1. This week: put the cap and daily limit in code; set the free tier to BYOK plus one capped trial debate (founder).
2. Starting now: about 5 live demos a week of 30 minutes with named professionals (20 over 4 weeks), written demo count by 15 November. Record once, cut clips. Log hours every Friday against 5 distribution, 2 support, 2 product fixes, 1 numbers.
3. **Retention gate, by 1 December 2026:** at least 8 of the first 30 users run a second debate within 7 days, and at least 3 show the screen to a friend unprompted. Fail: stop and rework the product.
4. **Payment gate (after the retention gate):** ask the first 30 for EUR 29; at least 8 of 30 pay, and at least 40 percent of payers run a second debate within 14 days. Only then test a higher price on a new cohort of 15.
5. **Day 90 after the first public link:** at least 20 paying strangers continues, at least 50 scales; fewer than 20 means Option C (personal tool or open source).
6. **Attention gate, by 1 January 2027:** 50 clips posted, median 500 views per clip, 2 percent click to signup. This checks the 5 distribution hours; it does not stop the product on its own.

**Cheapest test:** 30 live demos with named professionals, counting second debates, payments and who shows it to a friend, before any pricing page or ad.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 8 of the first 30 run a second debate, or fewer than 20 paying strangers at day 90. Then keep it as a personal tool or open source it.
**Revisit:** at the retention gate (1 December 2026) and at day 90, with the real counts.
