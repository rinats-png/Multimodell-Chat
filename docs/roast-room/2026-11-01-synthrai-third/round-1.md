## Hormozi

SEAT: Hormozi (Offers, pricing, and sales)
STANCE: Conditional yes (on B, with a paid pilot ahead of the public page)
OPTION SCORES: A 2/10 · B 7/10 · C 5/10 · D 2/10
PILLAR SCORE: 6/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: two-way
ESTIMATE: reached 15,000, payers 100 (about 70 active), niche. The report raises offer strength from 5 to 6 and demand from 5 to 5.5 because it gives people something they can pass on. Product love stays unproven until real reports exist, so the founder's "mass market" lean fails.

THE CALL: B, but run a 20-founder paid pilot first: a EUR 49 "tested decision" report, credited toward the first Pro month.
WHY:
1. A fails on the arithmetic (LTV:CAC, S070). These numbers are my assumptions: EUR 1 per click, 5% of clicks start a trial, 5% of trials pay. That puts CAC at EUR 400. Lifetime gross profit is about EUR 27 times 10 months, so EUR 270. That is under 1:1, and "the rule of thumb is that you want to have at least three to one on LTV to CAC." (S070). EUR 2,000 of cash buys five customers. Also, at an assumed EUR 4 per million tokens, the EUR 20 trial cap covers only about 80 trials a month, so ads would burn money against a wall.
2. Value Equation (S115): "10 minutes" cuts time and effort well. The weak spot is perceived likelihood, because nobody has tested report quality on real models. A paid pilot with a refund if the report misses buys proof (Guarantees, S030).
3. Client-financed acquisition (S037): "30-day cash must be greater than two times the cost of acquiring customer and the cost of fulfilling that customer." If you sell the EUR 49 front end by hand to founders you can reach, CAC is close to zero, delivery costs under EUR 1, and the rollover credit turns buyers into Pro subscribers (Money Model, S113).
THE RISK I SEE: EUR 29 a month for small-business owners lands in the dead zone of SMB pricing, and those buyers churn above 10% a month.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: fewer than 8 of 20 pitched founders paying EUR 49, or over 25% asking for a refund.
WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: configure payments, then personally pitch 20 founders the EUR 49 report with a refund if it does not change or sharpen their decision.
QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Name 20 people with a real money decision this month. Would they pay EUR 49 today?
