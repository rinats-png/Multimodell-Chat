# Draft resolution

**Recommendation:** Option B, focused launch around the decision report, behind a three-week paid gate. GO WITH CONDITIONS (interim B 7.1).

The product is sold first as a single **"tested decision" report for EUR 49**, refundable if it does not change or sharpen the decision, and credited to Pro. Pro at EUR 29 a month is the offer for people who come back for a second decision. A 3-pack for EUR 129 (Hormozi). Workspace stays off the public page. Reading a shared report stays free, without login (Naval).

## Conditions
1. **Production works** by 2026-11-15: payments, rate limiting, service and model keys configured; one full council session with report measured in euros, at or under EUR 1.50 (Buffett's number; Elon wanted under EUR 1).
2. **Paid gate**, 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-06 (three weeks; majority of Bezos, Graham, Hormozi, Buffett, Naval, Elon against Martell's five weeks): 20 hand-picked German founders and small-business owners with a real money decision, sold by hand. Pass: **at least 8 of 20 pay EUR 49** (Graham, Hormozi, Buffett), **refunds at or under 25%**, **at least 5 of 20 share their report unprompted** (Bezos, Graham, Hormozi, Elon), and at least 1 stranger signs up from a shared link (Graham).
3. **Cost cap tied to revenue** before any public link: platform spend for payers = the larger of EUR 50 or 20% of last month's revenue; the EUR 20 trial reserve stays separate and a test shows trials cannot draw from the paid reserve (Buffett). Own key offered as a discount, not the default for payers.
4. **Three councils visible**: business decision, pricing and offer, pre-mortem. The other ten hidden behind a flag, not deleted; any council nobody asks for within 90 days is deleted (Naval). Weighted majority 12 to 6 for three over one.
5. **Founder hours** logged weekly, at or under 10. Content by the Camcorder Method (Martell): at most 3 posts a week and 2 hours, cut from real reports and calls, starting in week 3 once real reports exist. No paid ads until an organic post reaches 3 times normal views (Gary V).
6. English interface and English landing page only after the gate passes.

## Next steps
1. By 2026-11-08: configure production, run 10 real council sessions, log cost and read every report out loud (Buffett, Elon, Sara).
2. By 2026-11-15: list 20 named founders with a real money decision this month; build the EUR 49 report checkout and the revenue-linked cap.
3. 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-06: sell and deliver the 20 reports; from week 3, 3 posts a week from the reports (with permission).
4. 2026-12-07: gate decision. Pass: public German B page with the report as the front door, English landing page next. Fail on payment or sharing: rework the report and run a second gate of 20; fail twice: Option D.

**Cheapest test:** 20 hand-sold EUR 49 reports to founders with a real decision, counting payments, refunds and unprompted shares.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 6 of 20 pay, or fewer than 4 of 20 share, or refunds above 25% at the gate; fewer than 40 paying buyers (reports or Pro) by day 90 after the public page.
