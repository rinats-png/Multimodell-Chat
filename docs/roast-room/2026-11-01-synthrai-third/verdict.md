## Why the council landed here

- **A decision is an occasional need, so sell the decision, not a subscription.** EUR 29 a month asks people to pay for a habit they do not have; a EUR 49 report, refundable and credited to Pro, collects cash today at about 98% margin (Graham, Startup = Growth; Hormozi, Churn math; Buffett, "how much do you get back"). All nine moved here in Round 2.
- **A paid gate beats a free beta.** A free user who forwards a link shows love, not willingness to pay; 20 hand-sold reports test price and sharing in three weeks (Hormozi, front-end offers; adopted by Graham, Elon, Martell, Sara).
- **Nothing counts until production works.** Sign-up, payments and live model calls are dead in production, and no real report exists yet (Elon, limiting factor; Gary V and Sara on marketing ahead of a working product).
- **The shared report is the only leverage.** The app can be copied; a scored decision a founder forwards to a partner carries the brand for free, so the link stays free and is measured (Naval, three kinds of leverage).
- **Broad is wrong on every number.** Paid ads give a CAC around EUR 400 against about EUR 270 of lifetime profit (Hormozi), the EUR 20 trial reserve covers 40 to 80 trials a month (Buffett, Elon, Hormozi), and 10 hours cannot serve a broad launch (Martell).

## Where they fought

- **Gate length.** Graham, Martell and Elon opened with a separate closed beta of 30 days; Bezos ("70% of the information"), Buffett and Naval wanted one to two weeks. Hormozi's paid pilot settled it: three weeks, 20 outside buyers, paid. Martell keeps five weeks only if the hours overflow (adopted as a stretch rule).
- **How many councils to show.** Six seats wanted three (business decision, pricing, pre-mortem), Graham, Sara and Elon one. Resolved: one during the gate, three on the public page, the other ten hidden and deleted after 90 days if nobody asks.
- **The cost cap.** Buffett found that the EUR 50 cap also covers paying users, about 6 full Pro users. All agree it must grow with revenue before any public link; Bezos's "EUR 50 plus 20% of last month's revenue" replaced Buffett's "the larger of".

## Conditions

1. **Production works by 2026-11-15:** payments, rate limiting, service and model keys configured; 10 real council sessions logged; cost per session at or under EUR 1.50 (hard cap), logged against a EUR 1 target.
2. **Cost cap before any public link:** platform spend for payers = EUR 50 plus 20% of last month's revenue; the EUR 20 trial reserve separate, with a test showing trials cannot draw from it. Own key offered as a discount, not as the default for payers.
3. **Paid gate, 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-06:** 20 German founders or small-business owners with a real money decision, sold by hand, EUR 49 per report, refundable, credited to Pro. Only the business decision council on the offer. Pass: at least 8 of 20 pay (close rate counted against everyone pitched), refunds at or under 25%, at least 5 paying buyers share their report unprompted, and shared links bring at least 1 sign-up per 20 views. Recorded, not a pass bar: whether 2 of the first 10 posts reach 3 times normal views. If 16 or more of 20 pay, the public page opens at EUR 79.
4. **Founder hours** logged weekly, at or under 10. If they exceed 10 in any two weeks of the gate, the gate stretches to five weeks with the same bars.
5. **Content:** Camcorder Method, at most 3 posts a week and 2 hours, from week 3, cut from real reports with permission. No paid ads until a post reaches 3 times normal views.
6. **English interface and landing page** only after the gate passes.
