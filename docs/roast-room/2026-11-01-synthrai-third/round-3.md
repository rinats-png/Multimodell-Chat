## Chair

1. **Cap = EUR 50 plus 20% of last month's revenue** (Bezos). Adopted. The "larger of" rule gives no headroom below EUR 250 of revenue; Naval argued the same sum in Round 2 and Buffett, who proposed "the larger of", did not defend it.
2. **Only the business decision council during the gate** (Graham, Elon). Adopted for the gate only. Unanswered: 20 buyers cannot test three report surfaces in 10 hours. Pricing and pre-mortem become visible on the public page if the gate passes, as the Round 2 majority wanted (Martell's line keeps three after the gate).
3. **6 or 7 of 20 paying is a fail on payment** (Graham). Adopted. The kill line moves to fewer than 8 of 20 paying, matching the pass bar.
4. **Shared-link conversion as a gate metric: at least 1 sign-up per 20 shared-link views, tracked from day one** (Naval, Gary V). Adopted. Gary V's "2 of the first 10 posts at 3x views" is adopted as a recorded signal, not a pass bar (Hormozi: small samples are signals).
5. **Share bar 7 of 20 counted among payers, kill below 5** (Sara). Partly adopted: shares are counted only among buyers who paid. The bar stays at 5, because Hormozi's point that 20 sales are too few to judge on shares went unanswered and four seats set 5 in Round 2. Sara's view goes to the minority report.
6. **Close rate counted against every founder pitched; if 16 or more of 20 pay, the public page opens at EUR 79** (Hormozi). Adopted, no objection.
7. **Gate stretches to five weeks with the same bars if founder hours exceed 10 in any two weeks** (Martell). Adopted, no objection.
8. **EUR 1.50 hard cap per session, logged against a EUR 1 target** (Elon). Adopted, no objection.

Final option scores: A 2.1 · B 7.9 · C 5.1 · D 2.0. Support for the resolution 8.0. Vote: 1 endorse, 8 endorse with amendment, 0 dissent.
