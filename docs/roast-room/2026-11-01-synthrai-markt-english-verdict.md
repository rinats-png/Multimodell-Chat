## Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS

**The call:** An English site does not make SynthrAI a mass-market product. It makes the same niche about 4 times bigger in reach and about 1.5 times bigger in paying users. Launch Option B as a two-market test: bring-your-own-key first, professionals who already check decisions with more than one AI, the German version (already built) live from day one, plus one English landing page and demo. Expect a side income of about EUR 2,600 a month, not a company.

## Why the council landed here

- Reach is limited by hours, not language. 10 hours a week buys about 30 clips a month at best, 4 demos and a lucky spike at worst (Martell, Elon, Bezos).
- English is more attention and much more competition. Every seat says conversion falls where free substitutes exist (Hormozi, Sara, Naval, Buffett).
- Broad on the platform key still breaks the money: free tokens cost cash before anyone pays (Buffett, Bezos, Naval, Elon, Gary V).
- The German version is already built, costs almost nothing to keep live, and is the only market with a measured conversion on the table. Seven seats moved to a two-market plan (Bezos, Graham, Buffett, Sara, Naval, Elon, Martell), Hormozi as a passive extra, Gary V as "two languages of the same clip".

## Where they fought

- **Does English change the answer.** Resolved: only the size. Payer estimates (45 to 150) closed to 60 to 110 after Sara's conversion argument and Elon's admission that 0.4% had no mechanism.
- **Is 10 hours enough.** Translation and legal pages are a one-off of 16 to 45 hours (Martell, Elon); the recurring costs are support across time zones, keeping two languages in sync and content. Content is about 30 clips a month, not 40.
- **Is German a better first market.** Hormozi: only as a free passenger, a second market with its own content fails. Buffett: it beats English-only on capital, but English viewers will not buy on a German site, so it is two products. Martell: German as an opt-in locale, no German content channel. The Chair adopts a sequence with measured conversion per page.

## Minority report

Gary V: volume is the lever and four swings a year is a lottery, thirty is a strategy; outliers are the only road past 500 payers and cannot be planned. What would have to be true: 3 of the first 30 clips reach 5 times the normal views. Hormozi: a second market without its own content and support loses. Graham: nobody has used the product twice or paid for it; clips build views, not lovers. Graham, Sara and Naval stress the English field is crowded; Buffett: the empty German field is a head start, not a moat.

## Conditions

1. Platform key capped at EUR 50 a month with a hard stop, plus a per-user daily limit, abuse-tested before any public link. Free tier: bring your own key plus one capped trial debate. One paid tier at EUR 29, no Workspace tier.
2. At most EUR 600 of the EUR 2,000 spent before the day-60 gate.
3. Support at or under 3 hours a week, weekly hours log from day 1. If content runs over 6 hours for two weeks, cut clips to 15 a month, not support.
4. English imprint, privacy, help pages and an English checkout wait until the day-30 gate passes. Check with a lawyer whether English-speaking customers need English legal pages from the first payment.
5. Email capture on both pages from day one; debate transcripts on the founder's own domain.

## What to do next

1. This week: put the platform-key cap in code; set up the German locale as is plus one English landing page and one demo debate; start the hours log; write the continue-or-C rule for day 90 and date it (founder).
2. Weeks 1 to 4: about 4 hours a week watching named professionals use it (the weekly hand-sent recording), 15 clips a month, camcorder videos for the 5 top support questions.
3. **Day-30 gate and decision date:** 10 strangers (not friends), at least 3 per language, each run 3 debates on their own key; 10 named people who would be upset if it vanished; 1,000 visits, 30 completed debates on user keys; 200 email subscribers. Fail: pause spending and rework. Fewer than 3 of the first 20 demo viewers asking for access: Option C.
4. **Day-60 check:** payers at EUR 29 per 1,000 visits per page; at most about 3 founder hours per payer; 3 of the first 30 clips at 5 times the normal views. If German converts above 1%, German becomes the primary offer test; otherwise split hours 70/30 toward the better page.
5. **Day-90:** at least 20 payers combined continues, at least 40 scales; fewer than 20 means Option C. If the English page has fewer than 10 of the payers, stop the English work.

**Cheapest test:** the first 30 named professionals, 15 German and 15 English, counting second debates, payments and who shows it to a friend.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 8 of the first 30 run a second debate within 7 days, or fewer than 20 payers at day 90.
**Revisit:** at day 30, day 60 and day 90, with the real counts.
