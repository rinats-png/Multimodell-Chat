## Chair
All nine seats voted "Endorse with amendment"; none dissented. Amendments, and what the Chair did with them:

1. **One retention number, 8 of 30 (Graham, Buffett, Elon): adopted.** The draft's gate (8 of 30) and kill line (fewer than 5 of 30) conflicted; both now say 8 of 30 second debates within 7 days.
2. **EUR 29 first, no EUR 79 cohort yet (Graham, Sara, Bezos): adopted. Hormozi and Gary V: partly.** Test EUR 29 on the first 30. Price tests come after the retention gate passes, and a higher price (EUR 79) is tried on a new cohort of 15 only after 8 of 30 have paid at EUR 29. Hormozi's wish to test the higher price early and Gary V's EUR 15 test are recorded in the minority report; no seat produced proof that anyone pays more than EUR 29.
3. **Payment gate 8 of 30 and 40% of payers run a second debate within 14 days (Hormozi): adopted.** 5 of 30 would be a 17% close rate and about EUR 145 a month; the better-argued number is 8.
4. **Sequence: retention gate first, then price (Bezos): adopted.**
5. **Demos spread over 4 weeks, about 5 a week; hours logged every Friday (Martell): adopted.** Elon's written count of demos held by 15 November is adopted as a check, not as a requirement to book all 20 this week. Naval's cap on demo calls within the 5 distribution hours: adopted. Martell's "20 pre-sold at EUR 15 or more": not adopted, it conflicts with the single EUR 29 price.
6. **Attention gate (Gary V): adopted as a secondary gate.** By 2027-01-01: 50 clips posted, median 500 views, 2% click to signup. Not a reason to stop on its own; it tells the founder whether the 5 hours are working.
7. **Sara's sharing signal: adopted.** At least 3 of the first 30 show the screen or a transcript to a friend unprompted.
8. **Hard stop on the EUR 150 cap (Buffett): adopted.** The platform key switches off when the cap is hit; it does not only send an alert.
9. **Debate transcripts public from day one; code release decided at day 90 (Naval): adopted.**
