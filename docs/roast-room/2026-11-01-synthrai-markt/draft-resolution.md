# Draft resolution: SynthrAI

**Recommendation: Option B, GO WITH CONDITIONS.** Launch SynthrAI as a focused product for professionals who already check decisions with more than one AI (founders, consultants, analysts, developers), German-speaking first. Treat it as a 90-day test of whether anyone pays, not as a mass-market launch. Say plainly what it is: a side income at realistic scale (about 60 payers and EUR 1,700 a month after 12 months), not a company with 500 payers.

## What it means
1. **Offer and audience.** Pitch the "second opinion before a costly decision". Narrow pitch, open landing page, broad-ish clips as the hook (Gary V).
2. **Access and cost.** Bring your own key (OpenRouter or provider keys) is the default. One capped trial debate on the platform key. Hard ceiling of EUR 150 a month on platform-key tokens plus a per-user daily limit, enforced in code and abuse-tested before any public link (all seats). Stored user keys stay encrypted, with a test that no other user can read them (Buffett).
3. **Price.** One paid tier, no Workspace tier, no token bundle. Test price on live demos: EUR 29 against EUR 79 in cohorts of 15 (Hormozi, Elon); never discount. The free tier stays BYOK plus one capped trial debate.
4. **Founder hours (10 a week).** 5 on distribution (about 6 clips from 2 recorded debates a week, plus demo calls), 2 on support, 2 on product fixes, 1 on the weekly numbers. No new features until 50 people have paid (Martell, Elon).
5. **Open source as a companion.** Publish real debates under the founder's name. Decide on releasing the code only after the 90-day gate (Naval).

## Gates (checkable)
- **Retention gate, by 2026-11-30 to 2026-12-01:** of the first 30 hand-recruited users, at least 8 run a second debate within 7 days (Bezos), and at least 5 of 30 pay or pre-pay within 30 days of a live demo (Graham). Below that, stop and rework the product before spending more hours.
- **Payment gate, day 90 after launch:** at least 20 paying strangers (not friends) continues the product; at least 50 means scale it; fewer than 20 means Option C (keep as a personal tool or open source).
- **Hours gate:** support stays at 2 hours a week or less, logged for 2 weeks.

## Next steps
1. This week: put EUR 150 cap and per-user daily limit in code; set the free tier to BYOK plus one capped trial (founder).
2. This week: record 2 real debates, cut 6 clips, post them; book 20 live demo sessions with named professionals (founder).
3. By 2026-12-01: first gate result. Day 90: payment gate and the decision on open sourcing.

**Cheapest test:** 30 live demos with named professionals, counting second debates and payments, before any pricing page or ad.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 5 of 30 second debates in week 1, or fewer than 20 paying strangers at day 90.
