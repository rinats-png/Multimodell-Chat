## Chair
Eight seats voted "Endorse with amendment", one (Gary V) "Endorse"; none dissented. What the Chair did with the amendments:

1. **Decision rule written in advance, day 30 is a decision date (Bezos): adopted.** The founder writes the continue-or-C rule before launch. A failed day-30 gate pauses spending and sends the founder back to rework; it is not argued into "one more month".
2. **Hours (Graham, Martell, Gary V): adopted as a staged plan.** Weeks 1 to 4: about 4 hours a week watching named professionals use it (the hand-sent weekly recording) and 15 clips a month. After the day-30 gate passes: about 30 clips a month. Weekly hours log from day 1 (Martell). If content runs over 6 hours for two weeks, cut clips to 15 a month, not support. Support stays capped at 3 hours a week.
3. **Day-30 gates added (Graham, Sara, Naval): adopted.** 10 strangers, not friends, each run 3 debates; 10 named people who would be upset if it vanished; 200 email subscribers. Fewer than 3 of the first 20 demo viewers asking for access means Option C (Sara).
4. **Money gate at day 60 (Hormozi): adopted.** Count only payers at EUR 29, at most about 3 founder hours per payer. If German converts above 1%, German becomes the primary offer test, not a 70/30 side bet.
5. **Cash and the German field (Buffett): adopted.** No more than EUR 600 of the EUR 2,000 before the day-60 gate. The empty German field is a head start, not a moat.
6. **English legal and help pages after the gate (Elon): adopted, with a limit.** Run the German page and one English demo first. English imprint, privacy and help pages, and an English checkout, wait until the day-30 gate passes. Martell's "English help page by day 14" moves behind that gate; his five camcorder support videos stay. Legal note: the founder must check, ideally with a lawyer, whether charging English-speaking customers needs English-language legal pages from the first payment.
7. **Scale bar 40 payers (Elon): adopted.** Day 90: 20 payers combined continues, 40 scales.
8. **Own domain for transcripts (Naval): adopted.**
