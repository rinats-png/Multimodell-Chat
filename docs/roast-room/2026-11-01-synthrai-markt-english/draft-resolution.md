# Draft resolution: SynthrAI with an English site

**Recommendation: Option B, GO WITH CONDITIONS, as a two-market test.** English does not lift SynthrAI out of niche. It makes the same niche about 4x larger in reach and about 1.5x larger in payers (median about 20,000 reachable and about 90 payers in 12 months, against 5,000 and 60 for German only). That is about EUR 2,600 a month at EUR 29, a side income, and the 500-payer goal is not reachable in any seat's estimate. Launch bring-your-own-key first, for professionals who already check decisions with more than one AI, with the German version already built as a second, measured market.

## What it means
1. **Two pages, one product.** German locale live from day one (built, about 1 hour a week to keep in sync, no German content channel). One English landing page and the debate screen translated (about 25 hours once, plus English imprint, privacy and help pages). Do not translate the whole app first (Elon, Graham).
2. **One channel for reach.** Public debate clips, about 30 a month, cut from 3 debates recorded each week. The same recording can carry both languages (Gary V). English viewers go to the English page; German viewers to the German page.
3. **Access and cost.** Bring your own key is the default. Free tier: one capped trial debate on the platform key. Platform-key spend capped at EUR 50 a month, hard stop, plus a per-user daily limit, abuse-tested before any public link (Buffett, Sara, Bezos). No Workspace tier. One paid tier at EUR 29.
4. **Founder hours (10 a week).** About 6 on content and demos, about 3 on support and product, 1 on admin and numbers. Support capped at 3 hours a week (Martell). Zero new features until 50 people have paid.
5. **Owned assets.** An email list and public debate transcripts, so the work compounds beyond the feeds (Naval).

## Gates (checkable)
- **Day 30:** at least 10 named professionals (at least 3 per language) have each run 3 debates on their own key; 1,000 visits and 30 completed debates on user keys (Bezos, Elon). Fail: rework before spending more hours.
- **Day 60:** conversion and payers per 1,000 visits measured separately for the German and the English page (Hormozi). Split the hours 70/30 toward the better one. At least 3 of the first 30 clips reach 5x the founder's normal views (Gary V).
- **Day 90:** at least 20 payers combined continues; at least 50 scales; fewer than 20 means Option C. If the English page has fewer than 10 of the payers, stop the English work (Elon, Buffett). Email list over 1,000 (Naval). Support at or under 3 hours a week with median ticket handling at or under 15 minutes (Martell).

## Next steps
1. This week: platform-key cap and daily limit in code; English landing page and one demo debate; hours log (founder).
2. Weeks 1 to 4: record 3 debates a week, cut about 30 clips a month, send one recording a week by hand to named professionals (Graham); do the German test first, since it needs no translation.
3. Day 30, day 60, day 90: the gates above.

**Cheapest test:** the first 30 named professionals, 15 German and 15 English, counting second debates, payments, and who shows it to a friend.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 20 payers combined at day 90, or fewer than 8 of the first 30 run a second debate within 7 days.
