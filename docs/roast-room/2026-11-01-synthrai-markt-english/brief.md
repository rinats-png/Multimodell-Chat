# Brief: SynthrAI with a fully English site. How many people would use it, and is it still a niche?

Date: 2026-11-01. Answer in English. Name the product "SynthrAI".

## What changed since the first council
A first council (German-language site, German-speaking first) judged this a NICHE: median about 5,000 people reachable in 12 months and about 60 payers (EUR 1,700 a month at EUR 29), all nine seats said niche, verdict GO WITH CONDITIONS on Option B (BYOK first, professionals, one paid tier, 90-day test). Its assumptions leaned on a German-speaking audience.

**New assumption for this council: the whole product and site are in English** (interface, landing page, content, support), aimed at a global audience. The founder is German-speaking but will publish and sell in English. Re-estimate everything from scratch. Say how much the English market changes reach, conversion, competition, price and cost, and say what it does NOT change (for example the API-key filter, the thin moat, the founder's 10 hours, no audience).

## The decision
Should the founder launch SynthrAI as a public paid product in English, and for whom? The Chair's main question: **how many people could plausibly use it and pay for it with an English-language site, and is it still a niche?** Every seat must give its own estimate of reachable users and payers in 12 months with the assumption behind it, compare it to the German-only baseline, and say "niche" or "mass market".

Options:
- **A. Public SaaS for a broad English-speaking audience.** Free tier plus Pro (EUR 29/month) and Workspace (EUR 79/month), platform key included, launch to everyone who uses ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
- **B. Focused niche, English.** Professionals who already check decisions with several models (founders, consultants, analysts, engineers, researchers), worldwide. Bring-your-own-key first, small paid tier, grow through content and demos.
- **C. Do not launch publicly.** Keep it a personal tool, or open-source it.

## The product
SynthrAI ("AI Discussion Room"): a group chat where several LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi and more via OpenRouter) discuss one question with the user and with each other. Each AI has a role, they disagree on purpose, a moderator picks who speaks, the app shows agreement or disagreement between models, and the user gets a result. Built and running: Next.js, Supabase with row level security, Stripe, glass design. No real users yet, no revenue. The interface text is German today and would be translated to English; own API keys are allowed in every tier. Translation and English legal pages (imprint, privacy) are small, one-off work.

## The business (all numbers assumed unless stated)
- Solo founder, part-time, no marketing budget, no audience, no existing English-language following.
- Plans (stated): Starter free 15,000 tokens a month; Pro EUR 29 for 500,000 tokens; Workspace EUR 79 for 2.5 million tokens.
- One debate with 3 to 6 models costs roughly 20,000 to 60,000 tokens (assumed). On the platform key that is a real cost per user.
- Competition (stated, from a quick search): several open-source "LLM council" command-line tools, the iOS app AIQuorum, AskQuorum, Suprmind, multi-model chat in OpenRouter's chat room, Poe and TypingMind, and the labs themselves. Almost all of these are English-language; the German-language field is nearly empty. So English raises the audience and the competition together.
- Market numbers the seats may use (assumed, not verified): hundreds of millions of weekly chat users worldwide; a few percent actively use two or more different models; professionals who check decisions across models are a smaller share of that; people willing to pay extra for a separate multi-model tool are far fewer. The English-speaking and English-capable pool is assumed roughly 10 to 20 times larger than the German-speaking one, but English-language attention is also far more crowded and ad prices are higher.

## Resources and limits
Cash: personal, small (assumed EUR 2,000 for hosting, tokens, translation and a domain). Time: about 10 hours a week. Decision within one month.

## Success
500 paying users within 12 months, or a clear signal of 50 paying users within 3 months. (assumed)

## The founder's lean
Wants the English version because the market is much bigger and believes this lifts it out of niche. The council should test that lean, not echo it.
